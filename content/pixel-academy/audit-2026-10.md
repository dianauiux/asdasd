## What works
1. The free webinar is the strongest 2026 post (reach, comments, shares).
2. Relatable series, the founder's "why us" and student stories had the best numbers in 2024.
3. The Oct 26 post has strong facts (≤10 students, mentor feedback, portfolio project, from 40,000 ֏). The packaging is the problem.
4. The AI angle is current.

## What's wrong
1. Two-year gap, no consistency. The 9k followers are mostly cold.
2. Three names (PIXEL IT School → Pixel Design Academy → PIXEL ACADEMY), pixelitschool.com, expired 2024 start dates on the grid.
3. Generic bio, no proof, no offer, no date.
4. Link on a vercel.app subdomain: 100 visits → 6 taps.
5. News reels (Figma, Claude) do nothing for the academy.
6. 7 courses in one reel, no date, no deadline: 0 saves.
7. No proof at all in 2026.
8. Reels hold 4.5–7 seconds: the hook doesn't work.
9. No comment or DM mechanic (when there was one, it worked).
10. Platform promo before launch, a 💙 in the caption, emoji-heavy captions, English hashtags.

## Funnel
Reach (teaching posts) → Trust (proof) → Lead (free mini-lesson / webinar / checklist via DM keyword) → Sale (one course, date, seats, deadline, consultation) → Retargeting (stories + small ads to engaged audience).
