## 1 · Tue 04.11 · Reel (English) · designer life

**Hooks**
1. "Can you make it look more modern?" ✓
2. The most dangerous sentence a client can say
3. I stopped accepting "make it modern" as a brief

**Script (25–30 s)**
- **0–2 s, on screen:** "Can you make it look more modern?" (big text, me looking at the camera)
- **Talking points:**
  - I hear this sentence on almost every project.
  - The client isn't asking for a new color or font. They feel something isn't working and don't have the words for it.
  - So instead of a new style, I ask one question: "On which screen do people stop?"
  - The answer is rarely the visual style. It's one confusing step.
- **Proof moment:** screen recording, 2 s before / 2 s after of one real screen (my own past project, cleared for sharing).
- **Close:** What's the sentence your clients repeat the most?

**Caption**
```
"Can you make it look more modern?"

That request is almost never about colors or fonts. The client can feel that something isn't working, but they can't name it yet.

So I don't pitch a new style. I ask: "On which screen do people stop?" That's usually where the real problem lives.

Save this for the next time you hear that sentence.

What's the line your clients repeat the most? Tell me in the comments.

#uxdesign #productdesign #designclients #saas
```

## 3 · Fri 07.11 · Reel (English) · client inquiry

**Hooks**
1. The question I ask before I open Figma ✓
2. I don't start with wireframes
3. Most redesigns fail before the first screen

**Script (30–35 s)**
- **0–2 s, on screen:** "The question I ask before I open Figma"
- **Talking points:**
  - Before any project, I ask the team one thing: what happens if we ship nothing?
  - If the answer is "not much," we're about to redesign something nobody needs.
  - If the answer is "we lose users at checkout," now we have a target.
  - That one answer decides the first screen I touch and how we'll know it worked.
- **Proof moment:** whiteboard or FigJam sketch of a simple funnel with one red drop-off step.
- **Close:** If you're building a product and want a second pair of eyes on it, send me the word AUDIT.

**Caption**
```
Before I open Figma, I ask one question:

What happens if we ship nothing?

"Not much" means we're about to polish something nobody needs.
"We lose people at checkout" means we have a target, a first screen and a way to measure success.

Same tools, same skills. A completely different project.

Save this for your next kickoff call.

Building something right now? Send me the word AUDIT and tell me what you're working on.

#productdesign #uxdesign #startup #saas
```

⚠️ **Decide before posting:** what does someone get when they send AUDIT? (For example a free 15-minute call, or a short written review of one screen.) The caption must not promise anything you don't offer.

**Caption**
```
Every time I open a new startup's website, I check the same 5 things in the first 10 seconds.

1. Can I tell what you do?
2. Is there a price, or at least a starting price?
3. Is there proof I can verify?
4. Does it work one-handed on a phone?
5. Is the next step obvious?

If two of these are missing, the problem isn't the design. It's the story the page is telling.

Save this and check your own page this week.
```
