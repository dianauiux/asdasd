# Dee Design · 10 face reels · no talking, filmed at home

Format: your face + on-screen text + trending audio · 6–12 s · English
Film all at the desk, same outfit, 3–4 reels in one 30-minute session.

| # | On-screen text | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client: "Can you make the logo bigger?" / Me, for the 4th time: | Slowly nod, smile, zoom the logo in Figma |
| 2 | POV: the developer says "that's not possible" | Turn to camera, raise one eyebrow, open the dev docs |
| 3 | Me at 9 am: "Today I'll design calmly" / Me at 6 pm: | Calm with coffee → hair up, 14 tabs open |
| 4 | When the client says "I'll know it when I see it" | Freeze, look at camera, close laptop slowly |
| 5 | 1:40 am in Yerevan. Everyone is asleep. New York is in a meeting. | Laptop light on your face, small smile, keep typing |
| 6 | AI: "Here's your design in 10 seconds" / Me, fixing it for 2 hours: | Look at screen, then at camera, deep breath |
| 7 | "Just a small change" | Count on fingers 1…10, give up, laugh |
| 8 | Things I say in every project: "What problem are we solving?" | Point at camera on each line (3 lines) |
| 9 | Me explaining white space to a client | Gesture "calm", then cover face |
| 10 | Rate this redesign 1–10 👇 | Hold phone with before/after to camera, then wink |

## Rules
- First frame = the text + your face. No intro.
- Text big, Clash Display Medium, inside safe zone.
- Caption: one line + a question. Example for #1:
  "How many times did you hear this one this week? 👇 #designerlife #designhumor #uxdesign"
- Humor targets the situation, never the client.
- Use your own footage only (no TV clips).
