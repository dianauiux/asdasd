## Caption
```
AI can design a screen in 10 seconds.

It can't tell you why users leave at step three.
It can't hear what your support team hears every day.
It can't decide what to remove.

That's the job now. Not the pixels. The decisions behind them.

AI is my fastest tool. It's not my replacement.

Designers: which part of your job can AI still not do? 👇

#aidesign #uxdesign #productdesign #designerlife
```
