# Dee Design · Follower growth content · Nov 2026

Goal: get non-followers to follow. English only, first person, no em-dashes, no filler words, no credential flex.

## What the data says (Oct 2026)
- Best reach and the only comments came from **designer-life / client reels**: "1 hour = 8 hours" (259 reach, 6 comments), "Same screens. Only one designer got hired again" (186).
- Single-image UX tips: 25–36 reach, 0 comments. Stop them.
- Reach is ~0.1× followers, so growth has to come from **shares to non-followers**.

## The growth formula
People follow when they expect **more of something they enjoyed**. So:
1. **Series, not one-offs.** A named, numbered series gives a reason to follow ("Part 2 tomorrow").
2. **Shareable first.** Relatable humor and "send this to…" posts travel to new people.
3. **Interactive.** Ask people to send their app or their worst client line, then answer in the next post.

## Three series
| Series | Format | Why it grows followers |
|---|---|---|
| **Client → English** | Reel, 10–15 s | Relatable humor. Designers tag each other and share. |
| **Fix it in 60 seconds** | Reel, screen recording | Viewers send their apps in comments → they follow to see if you picked theirs. |
| **Send this to your client** | Carousel | Designers send it to clients. New audience every share. |

## 3 · Fix it in 60 seconds · Ep. 1 · Reel (45–60 s)

**Hooks**
1. I fixed this checkout in 60 seconds ✓
2. One screen. Three fixes. 60 seconds.
3. Watch me fix a real checkout

**Before recording:** use a real public app screen you captured yourself. Don't name the brand unless it's a fair, current screenshot. Blur logos if unsure.

**Script**
- **0–2 s, on screen:** I fixed this checkout in 60 seconds (timer starts)
- **Fix 1:** Total price hidden until the last step → show it from the start.
- **Fix 2:** "Continue" → "Pay $49". People should know what the button does.
- **Fix 3:** 11 fields → 6. Ask only what you need to ship the order.
- **End:** Before / after side by side. "Want me to fix your app next? Drop its name in the comments."

**Caption**
```
One checkout. Three fixes. 60 seconds.

1. Show the total from the start.
2. Replace "Continue" with what happens next: "Pay $49".
3. Ask only for what you need. 11 fields became 6.

Small changes, but each one removes a reason to leave.

Fix it in 60 seconds is a new series. Drop an app name in the comments and I'll pick one for the next episode.

Save this for your next checkout review.

#uxdesign #productdesign #uidesign #saas
```

## 5 · Standalone reel · AI login screen

**Hooks**
1. I asked AI to design a login screen. Here's what I fixed. ✓
2. AI designed this. A designer fixed it.
3. What AI still gets wrong in UI

**Script**
- **0–2 s:** AI-generated login screen, full screen, timer.
- **Fix 1:** No visible password rules until you fail → show them upfront.
- **Fix 2:** "Forgot password" hidden in gray 10 px → readable and close to the field.
- **Fix 3:** Social login buttons louder than the main action → one primary, the rest secondary.
- **End:** "AI is fast. Taste and judgment are still the job."

**Caption**
```
I asked AI to design a login screen. It looked finished in 10 seconds.

Then I tried to use it.

1. Password rules appeared only after an error.
2. "Forgot password" was nearly invisible.
3. Three buttons were fighting to be the main action.

AI gives you a fast first draft. Judgment turns it into a product.

Save this for your next AI-generated screen.

Have you shipped an AI-generated screen without changes? Be honest in the comments.

#aidesign #uxdesign #productdesign #ai
```

## Growth habits (do these every week)
- **Every reel ends with a reason to follow**: "Part 2 next week", "Drop your app, I'll fix it next".
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Early replies push the reel to more non-followers.
- **Use Instagram trial reels** for experiments: they show only to non-followers first.
- **Collab posts**: invite one designer or developer friend per month to co-post a teardown. Their audience sees you.
- **Pin 3 posts**: best Client → English, best Fix it in 60 seconds, and the "Send this to your client" carousel.
- **Same cover style per series** so the grid shows a clear system.
- **Share every reel to stories** with a question sticker.
