## Ideas (face, little or no talking, filmed at home)

### Mom × Designer
1. **"My design sprint is 47 minutes long. That's one nap."** Timer on screen, you working fast, baby monitor on the desk, then you close the laptop when the monitor lights up.
2. **"Things my one-year-old taught me about UX"**: carousel. Tap targets should be big. If it's not obvious, they'll put it in their mouth (= users will misuse it). Repetition is how people learn.
3. **"1:40 am in Yerevan"**: storyboard ready (reel #5).
4. **"My office today"**: laptop + toy blocks next to the keyboard. Text: "Same laptop. New co-worker."

### Founder
5. **"I started a design school. Here's what nobody told me."** 3 lines on screen: students need feedback more than lessons; small groups beat big ones; your calendar is the real product.
6. **"Behind Pixel Academy"**: screen record of a lesson plan in Figma/Notion. Text: "What 4 months of design school looks like on my side."

### Mentor
7. **"3 things I tell every new student on day one"**: point at camera per line.
8. **"Your portfolio doesn't need 10 projects. It needs 3 good ones."** Before/after of a portfolio grid.

### Business woman

10. **"How I say no to a client without losing them"**: carousel with 3 real phrases.
