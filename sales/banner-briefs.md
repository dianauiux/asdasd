# Banner briefs for Claude (copy-paste)

How to use: paste **Block A** once at the start of a chat, attach 1–2 of your photos, then paste one brief from **Block B** per banner. Each brief is self-contained.

## Block A: master style prompt (paste once)

```
You are designing social banners for Diana Hakobyan (Dee Design), an independent product designer for FinTech, AI, SaaS and Web3 teams. Site: deedesign.me. Handle: @deeuxui.

FORMAT
- 1080×1350 px (4:5) unless the brief says otherwise. Safe margins 56 px.
- One idea per banner. Max 8 words in the headline.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Headlines: Clash Display, Medium (500) or Semibold (600), ALL CAPS, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -1%. Size 88–112 px.
- Small labels and captions: Inter Regular, 13–18 px, lowercase, letter-spacing 2–14%.
- One headline may be split across the canvas (one word top-left, one word right, one word bottom-left) to create an editorial rhythm.

PALETTE
- Electric blue #2E52B8, deep blue #17307C, light blue #9FB4E6
- Orange #F2591A, hot orange #FF7A2F, soft orange #FFB07A
- Cream #F4EEE6, ink #121212, white
- Each banner uses ONE dominant color (blue, orange, or cream) and one accent. Never all three equally.

VISUAL LANGUAGE (editorial, glossy, "digital agency" trend)
- Portrait photos are black-and-white, then tinted as a duotone (shadows deep blue, highlights light blue; or shadows dark orange, highlights pale orange).
- Motion blur or horizontal "smear" on portraits; ghost copies of the figure offset left/right at 25% opacity.
- Glass cards: white at 14% opacity, 1 px white border at 35%, backdrop blur 14 px, radius 22 px.
- Pill buttons: glass, text "explore the case study" or "swipe to explore" with an arrow → in a lighter pill at the right.
- Soft gradient orbs (orange on cream), glowing objects on dark fields (a glowing door, a laptop on a pedestal).
- Fine film grain over everything at 12–15% opacity.
- Header chrome on most banners: a small glass chip top-left with "DD" and a short bar; a thin arrow ↘ top-right (54 px, light weight).
- Small lowercase meta labels in corners (e.g. "research led", "data driven", "dev ready").
- NO orange outlines, NO glows around text, NO stock people, NO emojis, NO gradients on text.

CONTENT RULES
- Numbers are real and come only from the briefs. Never invent metrics.
- No company names on banners (only in captions if allowed).
- Tone: confident, calm, specific. No exclamation marks.
```
