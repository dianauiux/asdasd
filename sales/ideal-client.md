# Ideal client profile & lead scoring

## Hard filters (Diana's rules, applied before scoring)
- ✅ **Freelance / contract only.** No employment, part-time or full-time (contract-to-hire = caution).
- ✅ **Foreign clients only.** Exclude Yerevan/Armenian on-site or hybrid roles.
- ✅ **Fully remote from Armenia.** Exclude roles requiring residency in the US, EU, or UK.
- ✅ **Time zone fit (Diana = GMT+4):** EU/UK/Middle East is ideal; US clients only if async with ≤ 2–3h overlap (US East 9:00 = 18:00 Yerevan).

## Ideal clients
1. **Seed–Series B startups** with a product live or about to launch, and a founder or PM who owns design decisions
2. **Small SaaS companies (5–50 people)** without an in-house designer
3. **Agencies and dev studios** that need a reliable white-label product designer
4. **Established businesses** going digital (an app, portal, or dashboard) that have a clear business goal

## Green flags ✅
- A clear problem ("users drop off at onboarding") instead of "make it pretty"
- They mention users, metrics, or business goals
- The budget is stated and realistic, or they're open to a call
- The decision-maker is on the thread
- They have a dev team ready, so the design will actually ship

## Red flags 🚩
- "Quick and simple", "should take a few hours", or "$50 for a whole app"
- Asking for free test work beyond a small paid trial
- No clear decision-maker, or "my partner needs to approve everything"
- Wants to move off-platform before a contract
- Unrealistic timeline with a huge scope
- A client history of low ratings or many hires with no reviews (Upwork)

## Scoring rubric (0–100)
| Criterion | Weight | 0 points | Full points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget fit vs. our packages | 25 | Below the floor | At or above the Recommended tier |
| Scope clarity | 15 | Vague / "everything" | Defined problem + deliverables |
| Fit with Diana's strengths | 15 | Off-skill (e.g. pure illustration) | Product/UX core |
| Client quality (history, payment verified, reviews) | 15 | New, unverified, bad reviews | Verified, good history |
| Decision-maker access | 10 | Middleman | Founder or PM directly |
| Timeline realism | 10 | Impossible | Comfortable |
| Portfolio / referral value | 10 | None | Strong brand, great case study, or repeat potential |

**Verdict thresholds**
- **70+** → Pursue: personalized proposal today
- **50–69** → Pursue with caution: ask qualifying questions first
- **< 50** → Pass, or send a polite decline that offers the UX Audit as a smaller paid step
