# Sales playbook

## Pipeline stages
`New` → `Qualified` → `Contacted` → `Call booked` → `Proposal sent` → `Negotiating` → `Won` / `Lost` / `Nurture`

## Where to find work (weekly rhythm)
| Day | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Upwork / Contra | Send 5 tailored proposals to posts scoring 70+ |
| Tue | LinkedIn | Comment on 10 founder/PM posts; 3 warm DMs |
| Wed | Instagram `@dee_uxuidesign` / Medium | Publish 1 case-study or teardown post (see the dee-design-smm skill) |
| Thu | Cold outreach | 5 personalized emails with a micro-audit insight |
| Fri | Pipeline | Follow-ups, reconnect with past clients, update `leads.csv` |

Other sources: Wellfound (AngelList), Dribbble Jobs, Toptal/Malt (if accepted), Product Hunt launches (fresh startups that need UX), Y Combinator Work at a Startup, local tech communities, and agency partnerships.

## Discovery call (25 min)
1. **Context (5 min):** "Tell me about the product and who it's for."
2. **Problem (7 min):**
   - "What's not working today? How do you know?" (look for data)
   - "What happens if nothing changes in the next 3 months?"
3. **Goal (5 min):** "If this project goes perfectly, what's different in 90 days?" (get a metric)
4. **Logistics (5 min):** timeline, dev team, decision-maker, budget range:
   - "Projects like this usually land between $X and $Y depending on scope. Does that fit what you had in mind?"
5. **Next step (3 min):** "I'll send a proposal with 3 options by [day]. Can we book 15 minutes on [day] to walk through it?"

## Objection handling
| Objection | Response (acknowledge → reframe → smaller step) |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "Totally fair. What matters most to get right first? We can start with the Essential scope, or the UX Audit, and expand once you see results." |
| "Another designer quoted less" | "Makes sense to compare. My quote includes research, prototyping, and dev-ready handoff, so your devs don't have to guess. Happy to walk you through the difference." |
| "Can you do a free test?" | "I don't do free spec work, but I offer a paid UX Audit at a fixed price. You get real value either way, and it's a low-risk way to work together." |
| "We need it in a week" | "Doable if we focus. Here's what I can deliver in a week, and here's what follows in phase 2." (Rush fee applies.) |
| "Let me think about it" | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd need to feel confident? I can send that over." |
| "We'll do design ourselves / use a template" | "That can work for an MVP. If you want, I can do a 1-hour review of what you build so it doesn't hurt conversion." |

## Follow-up cadence (always add value)
| When | Message idea |
|---|---|
| Day 2 | Share a quick insight about their product (a screenshot plus 1 suggestion) |
| Day 5 | A relevant case study or Medium article |
| Day 10 | "Should I keep this open?", with an easy yes/no |
| Day 30 | Nurture: a new portfolio piece or a seasonal check-in |

## After a win
- Send the contract and invoice (50% upfront) within 24h
- Kickoff checklist: access, stakeholders, goals and metric, communication channel
- At the end: ask for a testimonial, a referral, and permission to publish the case study
