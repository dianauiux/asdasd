# Content bank: posts that get engagement (LinkedIn + Instagram)

**What earns engagement on LinkedIn for a designer, in order:**
1. **Teardowns of real public products** (saves + comments from founders)
2. **Opinions on current news** in fintech / AI / Web3 (comments, shares)
3. **Before / after with a number** (saves, inbound DMs)
4. **Hot takes about the design industry** (comments, reach)
5. **Mentor stories** (trust, shares)
6. **Checklists / frameworks** (saves)

**Rule:** 1 teardown every week. Teardowns are the single best client-magnet format.
**Rhythm:** Tue = value or opinion, Thu = proof or teardown. Both at 17:00–18:30 Yerevan.

---

## A. Teardowns ("How I'd fix this") ⭐ highest inbound

Pick a product your target clients use or envy: Revolut, Wise, Monzo, N26, Coinbase, Binance, MetaMask, Phantom, Notion AI, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Stripe, Robinhood, eToro, Trade Republic.

**Format:** 1 screenshot (banner) + 3 things you'd change + 1 thing they got right + question.

Hooks:
- `I signed up for {{product}} as a first-time user. Here's where I almost left.`
- `{{product}} has a $-billion valuation and a 7-step KYC. Three of those steps are habit, not law.`
- `The best onboarding in fintech right now is {{product}}. One screen is still costing them users.`
- `I opened {{AI product}} and it asked me for a prompt before showing me what it can do. Here's what I'd do instead.`

Post skeleton:
```
{{Hook}}

{{What happened on screen, 2–3 lines, concrete}}

Three things I'd change:
1. {{...}}
2. {{...}}
3. {{...}}

One thing they got right: {{...}}

{{Question: "Which fintech onboarding do you think gets this right?"}}
Save this if your product has a KYC step.
```
Banner: a screenshot with 1–3 orange annotation marks, FIG.0X frame.

## B. Opinions on news (comments + reach)

Search the week's news before writing. Verify the date and the fact. Examples of angles:
- A bank or exchange announced an app redesign → "what the numbers will probably show"
- An AI tool launched an agent / new UI → "the UX question nobody asked"
- A Web3 wallet got hacked or users lost funds through a confusing flow → "the UI was the exploit"
- Layoffs in a design team → "what gets lost when design shrinks" (no gloating)
- EU / US regulation on crypto or payments → "a compliance rule is a UX brief"

Hooks:
- `{{Company}} just shipped a redesign. Here's the metric I'd watch for the next 30 days.`
- `A compliance rule is a UX brief. {{Regulation}} will change your onboarding more than your legal page.`
- `Every AI product is adding an agent this month. Almost none of them tell the user what the agent is about to do.`

## C. Proof with a number (inbound DMs)

Use only cleared projects. Lead with the result, then one decision that drove it.
- `From 14 minutes to 6. What changed was not the UI, it was the order of the steps.` (Morpher)
- `Weekly active users +28% in 90 days. The feature was a Rewards Center. The design decision was where it lived.` (Asset Dash)
- `Support tickets saying "where do I find X" dropped 22%. We didn't add a search bar.` (Asset Dash)
- `An MVP in 12 weeks instead of 18. We cut scope with research, not with guesses.` (ALOA)
- `Mobile went from an afterthought to 40% of new signups in two quarters.` (Morpher)

## D. Hot takes (comments, reach, polarizing but fair)

- `Dark mode is not a feature. It's a test of whether your design system was built properly.`
- `Most "AI features" are a text box with a sparkle icon. Users don't need sparkles, they need to know what happens when they press Enter.`
- `A dashboard with 12 charts is a dashboard with no opinion.`
- `"Make it simple" is not a brief. "Get a first-time user to a first trade in under 6 minutes" is a brief.`
- `Your KYC screen is a trust test, and most products fail it by hiding why they're asking.`
- `A design system with 300 components is slower than one with 20 good ones.`
- `Founders don't need more screens. They need to know which screen loses the money.`

## G. Engagement mechanics (every post)

- **First line** carries the whole post. Write 3 hooks, keep the most specific one.
- **A number in the first two lines** when you have one.
- **One question at the end**, easy to answer in one sentence.
- **Save line** on value posts, none on opinion posts.
- **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes.** Ask a follow-up question in your reply.
- **Comment on 10 founder / PM posts before you publish.** Their replies bring their audience to your post.
- **Tag a company only when you praise it.** Never tag when you criticize.
- **Polls once a month:** `Which step loses more users: KYC or linking a bank account?`
- **Document carousels (PDF)** for checklists: the most-saved format on LinkedIn.
